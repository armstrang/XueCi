# Changelog

Every push of app changes to `main` makes a new release on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/armstrang/XueCi/releases)
(v1.0, v1.1, …). CI uses the `## v1.N` section below as the release notes for that version, so write the section
before pushing.

Test builds before a release are published as pre-releases v1.0-alpha-N.

## v1.12

**Every grammar pattern now says when to use it, and BCT gets its own study path.**

- **When to use it.** Every pattern in the Grammar book has a short note under its formula: the situation, how it
  sounds (polite, written, casual, annoyed…) and the one thing to watch out for. In Indonesian, English, Thai and
  Vietnamese.
- **Study tab.** The first tab is now called Study and has a small HSK | BCT switch at the top of Home. BCT has its
  own page with a word list, cards and a quiz, for BCT A and BCT B. It is extra practice: it never changes your realm
  or your daily plan. Your progress on these words is kept on the phone and is part of the backup code.
- **BCT A words.** 579 new business words for BCT A (greetings, time and money, office, phone and email, meetings,
  shopping, restaurants, travel, banking, orders and delivery, production, useful phrases), each with pinyin and the
  meaning in four languages. BCT B grows from 351 to 3,941 words: strategy and management, accounting and tax, banking and finance,
  import and export, logistics, contracts and law, marketing and sales, HR, manufacturing and quality, IT and
  e-commerce, real estate, meetings and letters, the economy, idioms and formal written Chinese, with pinyin and the
  meaning in four languages. The app fonts got 35 more characters for them.
- **Tests tab.** The page is only for exams now. Simulasi HSK starts with a page where you choose HSK 1–6 (your level
  is marked), the confusing "Unit" button is gone there, BCT A and B practice are one card where you choose A or B,
  and the business word list moved to the BCT page.

## v1.8

**Grammar in four books, in the order you learn it.**

- Four buttons on the Grammar page: HSK 3.0, HSK 2.0, BCT (Chinese for work, levels A and B) and YCT (the test for
  children and teens). A pattern found in several books shows in each of them, with its level there, and your
  progress on it is shared. HSK 2.0, BCT and YCT are put together after their syllabuses, not copied from an
  official list.
- The patterns of a level now follow the order you learn them in: sentence basics, numbers and time, 了 过 着,
  adverbs, comparing, verbs and complements, prepositions, special patterns like 把 and 被, joining sentences, then
  tone and expressions.
- 37 new patterns, among them 一边……一边, 刚……就, 直到……才, 每当……就, 不得不, 事实上, 说实话, 特别是, 通过, 难以,
  把……当作, 对于……而言, 针对, 与其……不如 and business patterns like 请您/麻烦您, 由……负责, 如有……请, 截至 and
  以……为准.
- Every formula was checked against its examples: the colours of each part now match the words in the sentence, a
  few formulas were corrected, and examples where the grammar word only hid inside another word (及 in 涉及) were
  replaced. Practice no longer uses such sentences either.
- About 2,350 new example sentences, five for every pattern of HSK 1–6, BCT and YCT. Each one is a real situation:
  two from daily life, one from study and one from work (polite, with 您 and 请), and a fifth that fits the book
  (school life for YCT, office life for BCT). Every part of the formula is marked in the sentence, with the Indonesian,
  English, Thai and Vietnamese translation, and a small label shows which situation it is.
- New in the Tests tab: **BCT A and BCT B practice**. Each is a paper in
  the style of the Business Chinese Test, built fresh each time from our own work sentences (meetings, calls, email,
  guests, meals, payment, trips, orders): listening, reading and sentence building, each section timed, scored out of
  100 with 60 as the pass mark, every question reviewed, and advice at the end. Full, short (a third), or one section
  only. It is practice in that style, not official questions, and says so. Answers are not written into your word
  memory. The banks hold 300 sentences for A and 800 for B.
- A **business word list** for BCT B in the Tests tab: 465 work words with pinyin and a meaning in your study language,
  grouped by topic, with search.
- The BCT book now speaks work: each of its 225 patterns has three more office examples (email, meetings, calls,
  deliveries, payment, business trips), and the BCT book shows only those. HSK and YCT books are unchanged.
- Mixed practice changes from round to round: mostly patterns that are fading, a few new ones and one or two strong
  ones, with sentences you haven't seen lately.
- Going back from a pattern to the list keeps your place instead of jumping to the top.

## v1.7

**A friendlier Grammar page.**

- Every grammar point now has a title in your own language, on the list and on the point page, so you know what you
  are opening.
- The formula shows what each part is (subject, verb, noun…) with its meaning and pinyin, in colours that match the
  example sentences. This works for every level, HSK 1–6.
- Every point has at least three examples. Each one is written word by word, with pinyin and meaning under each word
  and the full translation below, in Indonesian, English, Thai and Vietnamese.
- Short warnings where learners often slip, and the Practise button now sits above the examples.

## v1.6

**A grammar book, a much stricter placement test, and a red seal.**

- New Grammar page (语法) on the HSK tab, beside List, Cards and Quiz: the grammar points of HSK 1–6 after the
  official HSK 3.0 syllabus, grouped by kind. Each point has its formula, a short explanation in your language and
  examples you can listen to, mostly the app's own sentences with the grammar word marked.
- Practise any point: fill the gap with the right grammar word, or put the sentence's pieces in order. Answers are
  checked at once, and each point remembers how well you hold it (strong, growing, due again). "Mixed practice" takes
  the points of a level that need it most. Your grammar progress is part of the backup code.
- The placement test is now a real exam: 60 timed questions, no level labels, and "Don't know" or running out of time
  counts as wrong. From HSK 4 it asks you to type pinyin, pick the meaning of what you hear and build sentences; HSK 5–6
  want the pinyin and the meaning both right. Passing a level needs clearly more right answers than before, so a
  learner between HSK 4 and 5 no longer lands at the top.
- The tribulation has a new strike, 语: five sentences to put in order.
- Tests prefer sentences that show the level's grammar. The HSK simulation's advice names the grammar points behind
  the sentences you missed, and the realm guidance points to grammar that isn't strong yet.
- Sentence building cuts sentences better: names, numbers with their measure words and place words stay whole, and
  commas come with their piece.
- The Soul Sea button is now a red seal with your realm's name written top to bottom. It stamps again when you reach
  a new realm and has a thin gold line while the Soul Sea is open.

## v1.5

**A calmer speaking practice, one place for exams, and a black hole.**

- The tone check is gone. It didn't help enough, so it was removed along with its recording.
- Numbers heard as digits (10) now count as the hanzi you said (十), so a right answer is no longer marked wrong.
- The microphone's "ding" is muted while you speak and the media volume comes back right after.
- Mock exams are in one place, the Tests tab (HSK simulation and HSKK simulation, with your latest results).
  They are no longer a Quiz type or a card on the HSKK tab. Names are the same everywhere.
- The Soul Sea button is now a small black hole seen from the front, in cosmic colours: thick soft clouds of pink, lavender, violet and mint winding in towards a black core, thin streaks of light between them and a glowing ring that breathes. It turns once in about eight seconds and the clouds drift slowly inwards (still with light effects).
- The HSKK mock result shows what you said once, with missed hanzi marked; the triangle chart is gone.
- Home: "Today's Trial" has its name instead of 试, the goal line looks like a button, and the four stage bars
  of your realm now show your progress (they were never drawn).
- The Pinyin switch under a text is a clear pill.

## v1.4

**Speaking practice for the HSKK, a word widget, and a new tab bar.**

- The tab bar now has three tabs, HSK, HSKK and Tests, plus a round Soul Sea button beside them. List, Cards and Quiz
  are on the HSK tab, under today's plan.
- New HSKK tab with three levels: Basic (opens at HSK 1–3), Intermediate (at HSK 4) and Advanced (from HSK 5).
  You practise along the parts of the real test: listen and repeat, or listen and retell; read a text aloud; answer
  a question in your own words. While you read, every word lights up as you say it. Hundreds of sentences, texts and
  questions written for each level's own vocabulary, so the questions don't repeat.
- Tone check: after you repeat a sentence, the app can show the pitch of each syllable next to the right tone. It
  works on the phone, and the recording is dropped right away.
- HSKK mock test for each level, timed like the real one, with a score per part, everything you said and advice on
  what to practise next. The picture part of the real test is replaced by reading aloud.
- New Tests tab: the HSK simulation, the HSKK mock test and the realm tests in one place.
- Listening now asks the phone to work offline first and lasts as long as each question needs, not 15 seconds.
- The speaking quiz type is gone (HSKK replaces it). The microphone next to sentences in the List stays.
- Scores come from your phone's speech recognizer: a guide, not an examiner's mark.
- New home-screen widget for memorising: one word at a time from your realm's HSK level, with the hanzi, pinyin in
  tone colours and the meaning (the example sentence too when the widget is tall). Arrows move between words and
  units, and your place is kept. Paper look by day, night look in dark mode. Tap the hanzi to open the word in the
  List. Resize it to 2×3, 2×4, 3×2 or 4×2.
- If your phone has no Mandarin voice, the app now asks once whether to open the voice settings (at the start, and
  at the first speaker tap after each start). Settings has a Mandarin voice row too.

## v1.3

**Nature instead of music.**

- Music was still tiring to listen to while studying. Now each realm has a place instead: soft wind in the grass, a
  bamboo grove, a small stream, fine rain on a temple roof, a far waterfall, thin mountain wind, steady rain, and a
  clear morning in the mountains. No tune to follow, nothing sharp, very quiet.

## v1.2

**The music, made again: gentler on the ear.**

- The first music could be tiring to listen to. Every piece is new, after the calm music of the guqin and the xiao:
  one low, warm plucked zither and one low, breathy flute, never more than two voices, no beat, long silences
  between the phrases, nothing sharp or high, no bells, no glass, no big echo.
- Each piece is now 2 minutes 40 seconds long, so it no longer feels like a loop.
- Quieter: Soft, Medium and Loud are all lower than before. Off is still in Settings › Background music.

## v1.1

**Calm music for each realm, and a quiet living background.**

### Music
- Each realm has its own quiet piece, played on recorded instruments: a zither, a bamboo-like flute, bells, a soft
  gong and low strings. One short theme belongs to you; at each realm it goes a little further, and only at the top
  does it come home. Very slow, no drums, no build-ups: music to sit with, not to rush.
- On and soft by default. Settings › Background music: Off, Soft, Medium or Loud.
- The music steps back while a word is spoken, is silent while the app listens to you and during the HSK mock exam,
  and stays out of the way when another app is already playing music.

### A living background
- Slow motes of light and a thin mist drift behind every screen; your realm's orb breathes, one calm breath every six
  seconds.
- A right answer opens a soft golden glow; a wrong one leaves a thin ripple, like a drop on still water. Screens change
  with a light wash of ink.
- The tribulation no longer flashes lightning: each strike's sign rises softly and fades.
- With light visual effects, or with animations turned off on your phone, everything stands still.

## v1.0

**XueCi starts again at 1.0 as a native app (Flutter) instead of a web page inside the app: the same app and the
same progress, every screen drawn natively, with a new look that is easier to read.**

### Your progress comes with you
- Install it over the previous version (up to 1.17) as usual. On the first start the app moves
  everything by itself: word progress, memory model, goal, realm and its history, tests, mock exams, meditation
  days, reminder, theme and language.
- To be extra safe, make a backup code in the previous version first (Settings › Backup › Back up) and keep it
  somewhere. Its codes restore in this version, and this version's codes work the same way.

### Speaking practice
- A microphone next to each example sentence in the List, and a new quiz type, Speak: say the word or sentence and
  every hanzi turns green (right) or red (missed), with what the phone heard. Try again as often as you like.
- Tap the mic to start and tap again to stop (15 seconds at most; a ring counts down). You get a score from 0 to
  100 (Not yet, Getting there, Good, Perfect!), what you said, and each hanzi in green or red; tap a red one to
  hear it. The app listens itself, with no pop-up window. The mic breathes with
  your voice and shows what it hears as you speak. It asks once for the microphone; nothing is recorded or kept,
  only the recognized text is used. Strictness in Settings: Easy (tones may slip), Normal, Hard (only the phone's
  first guess).
- No Mandarin voice for the speaker yet? A short guide shows how to install one.

### A new icon
- Simpler: just 词, drawn as one clean line on seal red. With the phone in dark mode it turns night and gold (if
  your launcher follows the theme), and Android 13's themed icons show 词 alone in your wallpaper's colours.

### A new opening
- The app opens on its own background (no icon): a drop of gold ink, XueCi writing itself in letter by letter over
  a gold line, and Home rising in as the wordmark moves into its corner, in under two seconds. Tap to skip it. It
  plays only when the app starts fresh, follows your theme (light or dark), and is a short fade when the phone is
  set to remove animations.

### Cleaner, calmer screens
- Large titles with the level, unit and quiz type (as an icon) in glass pills under them; a tab bar with a name under every icon.
- Home: a small realm orb beside the realm's name and stages, then one big Today card with a single Start button,
  then word mastery.
- Quiz: the question alone in its card, the answers below where the thumb is (rows, or a 2×2 grid for hanzi and
  tones), a tick or cross on the answer and a light buzz, then one big Next.
- Cards: a bigger card and hanzi, bigger buttons. Swipe the card right for "I know it", left for "Don't know"; it
  tilts with your finger and springs back if you let go early. Sheets can be pulled down to close.
- Scroll a screen and its title moves into a small frosted bar at the top.
- Easier reading: bigger notes and explanations, long paragraphs aligned left, the breakthrough's strikes as clear
  rows with their score, the stats' bands in an even grid, and labels that never get cut off with large text (a
  shorter form appears instead). With visual effects on Low, sheets and bars are solid so nothing shows through.
- A tick or cross now springs onto the answer, with a light buzz when you were right; the Home percentage counts up
  as the app opens.
- Word mastery on Home is one calm line; tap it for the HSK lens and the counts.
- Tablets: Home sits in a centred column (upright) or two columns (landscape), with the HSK lens and the counts open.

### New look
- Glass all through: panels of soft milky glass that let a slow aurora of colour (your level's colour with warm
  and cool light) show through, a gentle glow along their edges and a bright rim where the light falls on them.
  The header buttons, the tab bar, menus and sheets are frosted glass over the page; the chosen tab, level and
  setting sit under a clear glass lens.
- Settings are grouped into one glass panel per section, like the phone's own settings; the realm guidance, the
  numbers on Stats and the answers in Quiz are glass inside glass.
- The space on Home goes above the realm orb; the realm line sits right above today's plan.
- Smoother: a new screen, card or question fades in on its own instead of being drawn on top of the old one; a
  button's spring redraws only the button; the frosted layers share one blur; shadows are drawn the cheap way; hidden
  meanings in the list are a soft bar instead of a blur; the Soul Sea opens several times faster; what the screens
  show from your progress is worked out once and reused until something changes; timers redraw only their clock.
- Little touches: the realm ring and the progress bars grow in, a right answer gives a small pop and a wrong one a
  short shake (with a buzz), and the word grid in Stats is drawn in one go, so it opens about twice as fast.
- Every text is checked for contrast against what is really behind it, on every main screen and sheet, in the
  light, dark and true-black themes (WCAG: 4.5:1, 3:1 for large text). Level, tone and element colours were
  darkened in the light theme where they were too pale to read.

### Changed
- The app's wording was rewritten to sound like a person, not a manual: shorter sentences, plain words, and
  explanations that say what to do (Indonesian and English first).
- Quiz: the Guided type and the Quick exam are gone (the HSK mock exam covers what the Quick exam did). Pick the
  type you want (Recognise is the default); today's session on Home still chooses the words and question types for
  you.
- The HSK mock exam was rebuilt after the new HSK 3.0 paper: the real number of questions per section, a clock for
  each section, and new kinds of question: hear a sentence and pick the word in it, its meaning, or whether a
  written sentence matches it (each sentence plays twice at most); put pieces in order to make a sentence; pick the
  character for the pinyin in brackets. HSK 1–2 show pinyin like the real paper. Choose the full paper or a short
  one (a third as long).
- Sentence gaps help by level: HSK 1–2 show the meaning and pinyin, HSK 3–4 the meaning, HSK 5 and up the sentence
  alone. Once a word is getting strong, the help fades. This help no longer costs the answer its credit; asking
  for the translation yourself still does. The placement test shows the meaning for HSK 1–4 too.
- After several misses in a row, a word you know well comes next, so a hard run doesn't turn into a losing streak.
- A "2′" button next to today's practice: about two minutes, six reviews, for days with little time.
- Thai and Vietnamese text rewritten to sound natural, like the Indonesian and English.
- About has a Licences section: the word list's MIT licence and the fonts' Open Font Licence.
- Terms of service, linked from About and Settings next to the privacy policy.
- 护法: an optional donation through Google Play (the coin next to Settings, and a quiet invitation once per realm after a breakthrough). It unlocks nothing; every feature stays free. Donors get a 护法 seal in the Soul Sea.
- Every screen is drawn natively instead of in a web page: Home, the word list, Cards, Quiz, the daily session, the
  HSK mock exam, Stats, the Soul Sea (its orb and the five-element ring), the realm sheet, the placement test and the
  tribulation with its lightning, Settings and the help guide. They work as before.
- Text follows the phone's text size, from small to very large, without breaking the layout.
- Backup codes are copied and pasted with the phone's own clipboard; sharing a code or the cultivation card uses the
  phone's share sheet.
- The learning engine (memory model, daily plan, question choice, realms, tests) is the same engine, rewritten and
  checked answer by answer against the previous version, so your plan and numbers stay the same.
- The list footer says that progress is saved on this device.

### Under the hood
- The app is now fully native. It still works offline and has no server of its own: speaking practice uses your
  phone's speech recognizer, and only donations talk to Google Play.
